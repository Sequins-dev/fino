BroadcastChannel — WHATWG-compatible one-to-many pub/sub across Realms.

Same-Realm and cross-Isolate delivery both flow through the Rust-side global registry (internal:broadcast). The registry fans out serialised message bytes to every subscriber on the same channel name, excluding the sender.

Delivery is always asynchronous: a wake pipe registered with loop.readable is used to defer delivery to the next event-loop turn.
